========================================================================================
L1 analogue: which backward letters preserve rare-side persistence, and the direction
========================================================================================
  A 3x+1: rare side lower;  a = 1 always works (prepending the smallest letter relaxes an upper bound)
      minimal admissible backward letter a = 1; p/m -> 2^a/q = 0.666667  =>  the step DECREASES the seed
  B 3x-1: rare side lower;  a = 1 always works (prepending the smallest letter relaxes an upper bound)
      minimal admissible backward letter a = 1; p/m -> 2^a/q = 0.666667  =>  the step DECREASES the seed
  C 5x+1: rare side upper;  needs a >= max_k (A[k]-A[k-1]) = 3 (prepending must beat a lower bound)
      minimal admissible backward letter a = 3; p/m -> 2^a/q = 1.600000  =>  the step INCREASES the seed
  D 5x-1: rare side upper;  needs a >= max_k (A[k]-A[k-1]) = 3 (prepending must beat a lower bound)
      minimal admissible backward letter a = 3; p/m -> 2^a/q = 1.600000  =>  the step INCREASES the seed

  So the well-ordering descent of L1 exists only on the lower side (q < 4).
  For q > 4 the persistence-preserving backward move increases the integer,
  and it is also NOT the move carrying the q-adic chain of L2 (that one is a = 1),
  so for C and D the two halves of the descent machinery decouple.
