  The excess is concentrated at SMALL n and fades with n.  Cause: at small n the
  modulus 2^{S_n} is tiny, so r_n is forced into a very small range and the block b_n
  is far from uniform -- the uniform-block null is simply a bad model there.
  By n >= 6 the observed rate tracks the null closely.

  So the earlier headline 'lift density runs above the uniform null' is CORRECT as a
  raw statement but MISLEADING as a finding: it is a small-n boundary effect, not a
  confinement effect.  Corrected here.
