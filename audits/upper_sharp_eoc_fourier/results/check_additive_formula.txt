[5] Hensel-truncation reformulation
    CITED [P]: Sturmian-Mahler Edge note, Proposition 7.1 ("Realizers are
    Hensel truncations"): r(D_N) = sum_{j<=S_N} eps_j 2^j, the integer formed
    by the first S_N+1 Hensel bits of the word's 2-adic point; it is odd
    (eps_0 = 1); and if 2^k <= r(D_N) < 2^(k+1) then eps_{k+1} = ... = eps_{S_N}
    = 0, a zero-run of length S_N - k below depth S_N + 1.  (The note states
    it for the mechanical/Sturmian point Xi_{alpha,beta}; its proof uses only
    the congruence and leastness, so the truncation statement is not special
    to that case.  Stated here as cited, for the mechanical case.)

    CONSEQUENCE, the restatement of the target:  r_min(N,0) <= 2^(cN) holds
    exactly when some 0-confined word of length N has r(D) with Hensel bits
    zero from position about cN up to S -- a zero-run of length about
    (alpha - c) N.  Checked here [V] as the elementary bit identity
        r < 2^k  <=>  bit_j(r) = 0 for every j in [k, S].
