P6  THE TERMINAL-TIP TEST, with the normalisation the brief requires
==================================================================================================
  Lemma T determines u mod 2^{d_{i+1}}.  The available window is w = S_N - S_{i+1} bits.
  ABSOLUTE information determined = min(d_{i+1}, w) bits.
  RELATIVE fraction              = min(d_{i+1}, w)/w .
  If the tip is special, the ABSOLUTE amount must rise with tau = i/N.

  Exact enumeration: N=11, S_N=22, digits 1..4  ->  154518 words
    i  tau=i/N  #eligible  window w  ABS bits det  REL fraction
    0    0.000     154518    20.000        2.0000        0.1000
    1    0.091     154518    18.000        2.0000        0.1111
    2    0.182     154518    16.000        2.0000        0.1250
    3    0.273     154518    14.000        2.0000        0.1429
    4    0.364     154518    12.000        2.0000        0.1667
    5    0.455     154518    10.000        2.0000        0.2000
    6    0.545     154518     8.000        2.0000        0.2500
    7    0.636     154518     6.000        2.0000        0.3333
    8    0.727     154518     4.000        2.0000        0.5000
    9    0.818     154518     2.000        2.0000        1.0000

  >>> ABSOLUTE bits determined is FLAT in tau (it is just E[d_{i+1}], the mean digit).
      RELATIVE fraction rises to 1 purely because the window w = S_N - S_{i+1} shrinks.
      The 'terminal tip' is the denominator, not the numerator.
