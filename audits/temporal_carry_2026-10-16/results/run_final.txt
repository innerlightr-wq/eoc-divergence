  My Lemma T+ applies to the CONTROLLED swap, where the continuation is NOT free.  These are

  >>> free suffixes spread the odd part over all odd classes: Thm D confirmed, and
      Lemma T+ does not contradict it -- T+ needs the controlled continuation.

P10  DOES ANY OF THIS CONSTRAIN POSITIVE-INTEGER REALIZABILITY?  (the decisive test)
==================================================================================================
  r_N is itself a nonnegative integer satisfying every congruence of the prefix.  So EVERY
  finite word -- confined, random, Sturmian, or an actual orbit prefix -- is realized by a
  positive integer.  A statement about finite prefixes therefore cannot exclude anything.
              family   N  S_N                    r_N  r_N realizes prefix?
     Sturmian/Beatty  30   47         71106568281083                  True
      periodic [1,2]  30   45         35184372088827                 False
         random 1..3  30   59     286387890844041901                  True
         orbit m0=27  30   42                     27                  True
        orbit m0=703  30   42                    703                  True

  (where False, r_N is even or the word is not orbit-admissible -- r_N still satisfies the
   CONGRUENCE, which is all the realizer condition asserts; admissibility is separate.)

  >>> Temporal differentiation is a statement about PAIRS OF FINITE PREFIXES.  Positive-
      integer realizability is a statement about ONE INFINITE word.  No amount of exact
      control on the former bears on the latter, because every finite prefix is realized.
