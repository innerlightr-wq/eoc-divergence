P0  model self-check: two independent implementations of C_N, and the identity
==============================================================================================
  C_N closed form == recurrence : OK
  2^{S_N} m_N = 3^N m_0 + C_N   : OK

==============================================================================================
P1  SINGLE PERTURBATION    d_i -> d_i + eps
==============================================================================================
     C_N = sum_{j<=i} 3^{N-1-j}2^{S_j}  +  sum_{j>i} 3^{N-1-j}2^{S_j}
     bumping d_i multiplies every S_j with j>i by 2^eps, so
         Delta_i C_N = (2^eps - 1) * T_i ,      T_i = sum_{j>i} 3^{N-1-j} 2^{S_j}.
  verified exactly on 15000 (word,i,eps) triples: OK

==============================================================================================
P2  SECOND DIFFERENCE  (the 'nonlinear interaction')
==============================================================================================
     With A = sum_{j<=i}, B = sum_{i<j<=k}, D = sum_{j>k}:
         C_N(e_i,e_k) = A + 2^{e_i} B + 2^{e_i+e_k} D ,  so
         Delta_i Delta_k C_N = (2^{e_i}-1)(2^{e_k}-1) * T_k .
  verified exactly on 66600 (word,i,k,eps) tuples: OK

  CONSEQUENCE (exact, no asymptotics):
      Delta_i Delta_k C_N  =  Delta_i C_N * Delta_k C_N / T_i .
  verified exactly on 7200 (word,i,k) pairs: OK

  >>> The interaction term is NONZERO but MULTIPLICATIVELY SEPARABLE.  It is the
      identity 2^{a+b} = 2^a 2^b and nothing more.  The second difference is a
      FUNCTION of the two first differences and the tail T_i; it carries no
      information absent from them.
